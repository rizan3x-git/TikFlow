1Introduction

TikFlow ("TikFlow", "we", "us", or "our") is an Android application that helps you download videos from links you provide. This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be collected, used, and shared when you use TikFlow, and the choices available to you.

By using TikFlow, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the app.

TikFlow is an independent third-party application and is not affiliated with TikTok or ByteDance.

2Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the app, the following information may be collected or processed:

  • Video links and request data: URLs you paste or share into TikFlow, and related information needed to process your download request.
  • Technical and device information: such as device model, Android version, app version, language, network type, and basic diagnostic information, which may be made available to us or to third-party providers when the app communicates with their services.
  • Usage information: how you interact with features of the app, which may help us maintain and improve it.
  • Network information: information such as IP address, which is typically transmitted whenever a device connects to an online server.
  • Information you send us: such as your email address and message content if you contact us.
  • Advertising-related information: if advertisements are introduced in the future, advertising partners may collect information such as advertising identifiers (see Section 8).

3How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Process your video link and deliver the download you requested.
  • Operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the app and its performance.
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, abuse, or misuse of the app.
  • Respond to your questions, feedback, and requests, including data deletion requests.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions.
  • Display advertisements, if and when they are introduced in a future version.

4Video Processing and Storage

When you request a download, TikFlow processes the video link you provide in order to retrieve the video. Downloaded videos may be saved on your device, in storage that you can access and manage. You are in control of the files on your device and can delete them at any time using your device's file manager or gallery.

Temporary processing data related to your request may be stored on external servers while the request is being handled. We do not claim ownership of any video you download using the app.

5On-Device and Server-Side Processing

TikFlow uses a combination of local and remote processing:

  • On your device: some tasks, such as handling the link you provide, saving files, and displaying your downloads, take place locally on your device.
  • On third-party servers: video URLs and related processing information may be transmitted to external servers operated by third-party providers so that your request can be processed.

Because of this, using TikFlow requires an internet connection and involves transmitting certain information off your device. Please do not submit links that contain information you consider sensitive or confidential.

6Data Sharing

We may share information in limited circumstances:

  • With third-party service providers that process requests, host data, provide analytics, or deliver advertising on our behalf or as part of the app's functionality.
  • For legal reasons, when we believe disclosure is required by law, regulation, legal process, or a valid governmental request, or is necessary to protect rights, safety, or security.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, where information may be transferred as part of that transaction.
  • With your consent, or at your direction.

Third-party providers that receive information may handle it according to their own policies, as described below.

7Third-Party Services

TikFlow relies on third-party services and servers to process video requests and may use other third-party tools in the future. These providers operate independently of TikFlow. They may collect, use, store, and process information according to their own privacy policies, which may differ from this one.

We do not control how these providers handle information and are not responsible for their practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services involved. Third-party services currently or in the future associated with the app may include Google services such as Google AdMob (see Section 8).

8Advertisements

TikFlow does not currently display advertisements, but the app currently plans to introduce advertisements in future versions. We may use Google AdMob or similar advertising services for this purpose.

If advertisements are introduced, advertising providers such as Google AdMob may collect and use certain information, such as device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP address, and information about your interactions with ads, to serve and measure ads and to provide personalized or non-personalized advertising where permitted. Their use of information is governed by their own privacy policies. Where required by law, we will request your consent, and we will update this Privacy Policy before such features are released. You can typically manage advertising preferences, including resetting or opting out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings.

9Data Retention

Videos you download remain on your device until you delete them. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

Temporary processing data may be stored on external servers, and the length of time it is kept may depend on the third-party providers involved and their own retention practices. Please refer to those providers' privacy policies for details.

10Data Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect information handled through the app. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We also cannot guarantee the security practices of third-party providers. You use the app at your own risk and are responsible for keeping your device secure.

11User Rights and Data Deletion

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your information, which may include the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of it, or to object to certain processing.

  • On-device data: you can delete downloaded videos and clear app data at any time through your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Information we hold: you may contact us to request access to or deletion of information associated with you that is within our control.
  • Third-party providers: for information held by third parties, you may need to contact those providers directly under their own privacy policies.

To make a request, email us at your-email@example.com. We may need to verify your request and will respond as required by applicable law.

12Children's Privacy

TikFlow is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required by the laws of your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to address it.

13External Links and Platforms

TikFlow works with links to content hosted on external platforms and may contain links to other websites or services. We do not own or control those platforms and are not responsible for their content, privacy practices, or terms. Their handling of your information is governed by their own policies, and we recommend that you review them.

When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Your continued use of TikFlow after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
